About RipAudit

RipAudit compares each movie file's measured runtime with an independent reference and flags differences for review. It reads media files read-only and never modifies, renames, deletes, or transcodes them.

What the results mean

  • No runtime issue detected means the measured duration is within tolerance of the reference. It does not prove that every frame, track, or extra is present.
  • TMDB lists one general runtime per movie, which may describe a different cut than your disc. Your own source runtime, when entered, is used instead.
  • Full-file decode checking is Tdarr's job. RipAudit does not import Tdarr results yet.
